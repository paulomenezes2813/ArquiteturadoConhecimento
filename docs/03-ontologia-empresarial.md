# Ontologia empresarial — 12 domínios

**A. Estrutura organizacional:** Organization, OrganizationalUnit, Department, Position, Role, Person, Membership, Team, Responsibility, Authority, OrganizationalStructure.

**B. Operações e processos:** Process, ProcessVersion, Activity, Task, Workflow, WorkflowTransition, ProcessInstance, TaskExecution, Trigger, InputOutput, SLA, Exception.

**C. Conhecimento institucional:** KnowledgeObject, KnowledgeVersion, Concept, Claim, Rule, Policy, Procedure, Instruction, Standard, Guideline, BestPractice, KnowledgeDomain, KnowledgeDependency.

**D. Decisões e governança:** Decision, DecisionAlternative, DecisionRationale, Approval, ApprovalWorkflow, Delegation, ChangeRequest, Conflict, ConflictResolution, Review, AuditEvent.

**E. Evidências e fontes:** Source, Artifact, ArtifactVersion, ContentSegment, Transcript, Extraction, Citation, EvidenceLink, Meeting, MeetingRecord.

**F. Pessoas, competências e aprendizagem:** Competency, Skill, Experience, LessonLearned, Hypothesis, Experiment, Finding, ImprovementProposal, Training, Qualification.

**G. Produtos, serviços e ativos:** Product, Service, Asset, Equipment, Facility, Location, Component, Configuration, AssetModel, MaintenancePlan, AssetLifecycleEvent.

**H. Relacionamentos comerciais:** Party, Customer, Supplier, Partner, Contract, ContractClause, Obligation, Agreement, PurchaseOrder, ServiceCommitment.

**I. Recursos e finanças:** Resource, Budget, CostCenter, ExpenseCategory, RevenueCategory, FinancialTransactionReference, Allocation, FinancialIndicator.

**J. Riscos, controles e conformidade:** Risk, Control, ComplianceRequirement, LegalRequirement, Incident, NonConformity, CorrectiveAction, PreventiveAction, AuditFinding.

**K. Desempenho e resultados:** Indicator, IndicatorDefinition, Measurement, Target, Objective, KeyResult, PerformanceAssessment, Observation.

**L. Tecnologia e agentes:** System, Integration, API, DataSource, AIModel, Agent, AgentVersion, AgentCapability, ToolPermission, AgentExecution, KnowledgeAccessPolicy.

## Extensibilidade
O núcleo é universal; extensões setoriais (hotelaria, educação, indústria, saúde, jurídico) não devem poluir a ontologia básica. Nem toda classe ontológica precisa de tabela física na primeira versão.
